## What it looks like

Comparison uses another person's choices as a ruler for your worth. The named person may be a coworker, sibling, ex, friend, or the always-convenient "everyone else."

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"Your cousin never makes this difficult."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"Other people manage without complaining."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"I wish you had Maya's attitude."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The bait is competition. The real request is usually hiding underneath it.

## Why it works on you

Comparison hooks rank and belonging. You may start proving you are as good as the comparison person, which means you are now playing on their board.

The pattern is not learning from someone else. Examples can be helpful. The pattern is using someone else as a stick.

## The tell

Watch for your answer being measured against a person instead of the actual facts: your time, values, role, money, or limits.

<ScriptBlock label="Anchor line">
  <p>"I am not comparing myself to them. My answer is about this request."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="When they cite someone else's choice">
  <p>"Their choice does not decide mine."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="When they make it a contest">
  <p>"I am staying with what I can do, not competing with someone else."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The tell is pressure that needs a rival to function.

## The trap

The trap is defending your ranking.

<ScriptBlock label="The line that feeds it">
  <p>"Maya only does that because she has more help, and I work just as hard as she does."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

Maybe true. Also exactly the contest they invited you into.

<ScriptBlock label="Better">
  <p>"Maya can make her choice. I am making mine."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Exit line">
  <p>"I am not going to discuss this as a comparison."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

## Counters

Reframe returns the talk to the request. Boundary stops the scoreboard. Grey Rock helps when the comparison is bait for reaction.

<ScriptBlock label="Reframe">
  <p>"The useful question is what I can commit to, and this is what I can commit to."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Grey rock">
  <p>"Noted. My answer is still no."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Smallest usable version">
  <p>"That is not my measure."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The win is not becoming the better comparison. The win is leaving the comparison frame.
