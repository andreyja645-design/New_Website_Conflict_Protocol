## What it looks like

An empathy trap starts with real or claimed distress, then turns your compassion into an obligation to rescue, absorb, or abandon your limit.

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"I thought you would understand."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"I am barely holding it together, and you are choosing now to say no?"</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="They say">
  <p>"If you had any heart, you would help me."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

Their pain becomes the whole weather system. Your boundary gets treated like bad manners.

## Why it works on you

Empathy traps hook your kindness. You may be able to feel their distress and still be unable to take over the problem. That distinction can disappear fast under pressure.

The pattern is not someone needing care. The pattern is using need to bypass consent, capacity, or responsibility.

## The tell

Watch for compassion being turned into a required action: if you feel for them, you must do the thing.

<ScriptBlock label="Anchor line">
  <p>"I am sorry this is hard, and I am still not able to do it."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="When the pain is real">
  <p>"I care about what you are carrying. My boundary stays the same."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="When you can offer a small thing">
  <p>"I can listen for a few minutes, but I cannot take over the problem."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The tell is when empathy is treated as surrender.

## The trap

The trap is rescuing to prove you are not heartless.

<ScriptBlock label="The line that feeds it">
  <p>"Okay, I cannot stand seeing you like this. I will handle it."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

That may soothe the moment and train the pattern. Your compassion deserves a boundary too.

<ScriptBlock label="Better">
  <p>"I can care about you and still not be the person who solves this."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Exit line">
  <p>"I am not going to keep discussing my heart as the reason I should say yes."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

## Counters

Validation honors the distress. Boundary protects capacity. Broken Record keeps the answer steady when the emotional volume rises.

<ScriptBlock label="Validate and hold">
  <p>"That sounds painful. I am still not able to take it on."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Repeat">
  <p>"I cannot take over this problem."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

<ScriptBlock label="Smallest usable version">
  <p>"I care, and I cannot do it."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The win is not caring less. The win is caring without disappearing.
