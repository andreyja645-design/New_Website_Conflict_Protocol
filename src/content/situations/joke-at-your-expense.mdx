## The scene

You are at dinner, or in the group chat, or standing near the kitchen island while someone does that little performer voice.

**Friend:** "Do not ask them to pick the restaurant. We will be here until next year."

People laugh. Not cruelly, maybe. Enough.

**You:** "Hey, do not do that."

**Friend:** "Relax. It was a joke. Everyone laughed."

And now you are trapped between two bad options: swallow it and feel small, or object and become the person who "made it weird."

## What's actually happening

This is [public shaming](/patterns/public-shaming/) in a casual outfit, plus [minimizing](/patterns/minimizing/) when they call it "just a joke."

The social pressure is the point. The audience makes you want to smooth it over. The laugh becomes evidence. But a group laugh is not a court ruling. It is just a sound people make when the room gives them a cue.

<ScriptBlock label="Anchor">
  <p>"Do not make me the joke like that."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

Short. Plain. Said once, it changes the room's weather.

## The script

**Friend:** "Careful, they are very delicate about everything."

<ScriptBlock label="You">
  <p>"Do not make me the joke like that."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

You are not explaining your childhood. You are stopping the public bit.

**Friend:** "Oh come on. Everyone laughed."

<ScriptBlock label="You">
  <p>"I am not asking everyone to vote. I am telling you not to aim that joke at me."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

That is the whole move: the audience pressure gets removed.

**Friend:** "Wow, mood killer."

<ScriptBlock label="You">
  <p>"I am fine with jokes. I am not fine being the punchline."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

Specific. No "I never said jokes are illegal" spiral.

**Friend:** "It was not that deep."

<ScriptBlock label="You">
  <p>"It does not need to be deep for me to ask you to stop."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

Minimizing wants you to prove depth. Nope.

**Friend:** "Fine, I will never joke again."

<ScriptBlock label="You">
  <p>"You can joke. Do not joke about me like that."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

The fake extreme gets clipped back to the actual request.

## Variations

If you want to answer in the moment:

<ScriptBlock label="Live">
  <p>"Not that one."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

If you want to wait until later:

<ScriptBlock label="Afterward">
  <p>"At dinner, the joke about me landed badly. Do not use me as the punchline like that again."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

If they say you embarrassed them:

<ScriptBlock label="Reversal">
  <p>"I did not embarrass you by asking you to stop. The joke put me on the spot first."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

If the group piles on:

<ScriptBlock label="Group">
  <p>"This is not a group vote. I am setting a limit about jokes aimed at me."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

If they keep doing it:

<ScriptBlock label="Consequence">
  <p>"If the jokes keep being at my expense, I am going to step out of the conversation."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

If you froze and laughed along:

<ScriptBlock label="Reset">
  <p>"I laughed in the moment because I felt put on the spot. I did not actually like that joke."</p>
</ScriptBlock>

This page is not about whether you are too sensitive. That is the deflection page. This one is about the social stage: the laugh, the witnesses, the tiny panic of being expected to play along while you are being reduced.
